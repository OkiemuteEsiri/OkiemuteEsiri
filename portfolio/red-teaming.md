# Red Teaming

## Purpose

Demonstrate a disciplined adversary-simulation methodology for authorized environments. The objective is to validate attack paths, control effectiveness, detection capability and remediation priorities rather than simply obtain access.

## Engagement Lifecycle

1. Rules of engagement and authorization
2. Intelligence gathering and reconnaissance
3. Attack-surface mapping
4. Initial-access validation
5. Privilege-escalation analysis
6. Credential and identity attack-path assessment
7. Lateral-movement validation
8. Objective / impact validation
9. Detection and response observation
10. Evidence handling, reporting and remediation guidance

## Areas of Focus

- External and internal reconnaissance
- Network and service enumeration
- Web and API attack surfaces
- Active Directory attack paths
- Credential exposure and privilege boundaries
- Segmentation and trust relationships
- Misconfiguration chains
- Detection opportunities mapped to adversary behavior

## Tooling

Nmap · Burp Suite · Metasploit · BloodHound · Kerbrute · Impacket · Kali Linux · PowerShell · Python

## Reporting Standard

Each finding should include:

- Finding title and affected component
- Severity and business impact
- Preconditions and attack path
- Evidence sufficient to reproduce in an authorized environment
- Detection opportunities
- Root cause
- Tactical remediation
- Strategic control improvement
- Retest status

## Ethics & Scope

All activity represented in this portfolio is limited to systems I own, intentionally vulnerable labs, CTF environments, or environments where explicit authorization has been granted.
