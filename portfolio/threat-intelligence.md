# Threat Intelligence

## Objective

Turn raw vulnerability and adversary information into prioritized defensive decisions.

## Intelligence Workflow

1. Define intelligence requirements.
2. Collect from trusted internal and external sources.
3. Normalize CVEs, indicators, affected technologies and actor behavior.
4. Assess source confidence and relevance.
5. Correlate intelligence with internal asset and exposure data.
6. Prioritize action based on exploitability and business context.
7. Disseminate concise technical and executive outputs.
8. Review whether the intelligence resulted in measurable risk reduction.

## Priority Signals

- Known exploitation in the wild
- Exploit proof-of-concept availability
- Weaponization or exploitation maturity
- Threat-actor targeting patterns
- Internet-facing vulnerable technology
- High-value or privileged systems
- Newly disclosed critical vulnerabilities
- Vendor mitigation availability
- Detection opportunities

## Vulnerability Intelligence Use Case

```text
External CVE intelligence
        +
Known-exploitation evidence
        +
Internal asset inventory
        +
Business criticality
        +
Existing controls
        ↓
Actionable remediation priority
```

## Outputs

- Vulnerability intelligence briefs
- Exploitability assessments
- Priority remediation advisories
- Threat summaries for stakeholders
- Detection recommendations
- Exposure watchlists

## Principles

Source confidence, timeliness, relevance and internal context matter more than volume. Intelligence is useful only when it supports a decision or defensive action.
